Purpose of the game

Objective

Score the greatest total value in your personal holdings after five years. Your holdings consist of your face-up Plot and face-down Cellar. Every surviving card scores its printed rank.

Players cooperate to complete fields, but rewards belong to individual players and failed fields expose private holdings to requisition.

What you need

Components

  • 52 crop cards: Wheat, Sunflower, Potato, and Beet, ranked Ace through King.
  • 1 Saboteur card, ranked 0 and marked with all four crops.
  • 4 Medal cards.
  • 1 double-sided Central Planner reference card.
  • This rule leaflet or the rules on this page.

Card values

Ace = 1, numbered cards use their number, Jack = 11, Queen = 12, King = 13, and the Saboteur = 0. Printed rank is both work value and final score.

Before Year 1

Setup

  1. Set aside all four Aces. For each crop, shuffle its 2, 3, 4, and 5 into a separate face-down reward pile beside that crop’s field.
  2. Shuffle the 6 through King of every crop together with the Saboteur to form the 33-card Workers Deck.
  3. Give each player space for a face-up Plot and a face-down Cellar. Only a Cellar’s owner may inspect its cards.
  4. Reserve a public area called the North. Cards sent there remain face-up and out of play.
  5. Shuffle the four Aces and deal one face-down into each player’s Cellar. Reveal the Ace of Beet, move it face-up to its owner’s Plot, and give that player the Central Planner card. If nobody received it in a three-player game, reveal and move the Ace of Wheat instead and use its owner.
  6. Place all four Medal cards beside the play area. Once trump is set, you may keep the supply beside the trump crop’s field as a reminder, or off to one side with no trump.

One round

The yearly sequence

  1. Deal and reveal the year’s four rewards in Years 1–4; Year 5 has no rewards.
  2. Resolve permitted Planner reward swaps, determine trump, then resolve optional hand swaps.
  3. Play four tricks—or three tricks in Year 5.
  4. After each trick, assign every card and check for completed fields.
  5. Bank the final card in each player’s hand.
  6. Resolve requisition.
  7. Clean up and pass the Central Planner left.

Start of a year

Planning

Years 1–4

  1. The Planner deals five cards face-down to each player.
  2. Reveal the top card of each crop’s reward pile, keeping it face-up on top of its pile.
  3. For each revealed reward, the Planner may replace it with a card of the same crop from their hand, at most once per field. The replaced reward joins the Planner’s hand and is played as a Worker. With three players, no reward swaps occur in Year 4. The Saboteur counts as every crop and may become any field’s reward; claimed as a reward, it scores 0 and does not make the field fail.
  4. With four players, the Planner chooses trump in Years 1–4. With three players, the Planner chooses trump in Years 1–3; in Year 4, reveal and send North the top Worker, whose crop becomes trump. If it is the Saboteur, there is no trump.

Planner reward-swap example

The Wheat 3 is revealed as Wheat’s reward. If the Planner holds the Wheat 9, they may make the Wheat 9 the new reward and take the Wheat 3 into their hand.

Swapping in Years 1–5

Beginning in Year 1, each player other than the Central Planner may make exactly one optional swap before the first trick. Exchange one card from your hand with one card in your own Plot or Cellar. The Planner may also swap in Year 5—or in Years 4 and 5 with three players. In chosen-trump years, start to the Planner’s left and continue clockwise, skipping the Planner. In revealed-trump years, the Planner goes first and the swap continues to the left. Each player starts with an Ace to swap.

  • You may inspect your own Cellar before choosing.
  • A hand card replacing a Plot card is placed face-up.
  • A hand card replacing a Cellar card is placed face-down.
  • The card taken from the Plot or Cellar joins your hand and may be played normally.

Trick-taking

  1. The player to the Planner’s left leads the first trick.
  2. Play continues clockwise, one card each. Follow the led crop if able.
  3. The Saboteur may always be played; if you hold it but no other card of the led crop, you must play it.
  4. A player unable to follow may play any card, including trump.
  5. The highest trump wins. If no trump was played, the highest card of the led crop wins.
  6. The winner becomes the Brigadier, takes one Medal, and assigns the trick.
  7. After assignment, that Brigadier leads the next trick.

After every trick

Assigning Workers

The Brigadier assigns every card from the completed trick, one at a time. A card may go to any field whose crop appeared anywhere in that trick.

  • A card does not have to be assigned to its own crop.
  • Cards in one trick may be split across several legal fields.
  • Assigned Workers stay face-up until the end of the year, so every field’s total is public.
  • Previously completed fields remain legal assignment targets.

Example

A trick contains three Wheat cards and one Beet card. Every card in that trick may be assigned to Wheat or Beet in any combination.

Crossing the threshold

Completing fields

Add the printed ranks of all cards assigned to each field. A field completes as soon as it reaches its quota: 40 hours with four players or 32 hours with three.

The Brigadier whose assignment crosses the quota immediately takes that field’s visible reward and places it face-up in their Plot. Each field pays at most one reward per year in Years 1–4. Year 5 has no rewards, but fields are still worked and can fail.

The field stays open for later assignments. Reaching quota does not protect it from a Saboteur assigned later in the same year.

Rank 0 · all four crops

The Saboteur

Following and winning tricks

  • The Saboteur counts as every crop. You may always play it, even when holding other cards of the led crop; you must play it if you have no other card of that crop.
  • It is a rank-0 trump regardless of the chosen trump crop.
  • It beats every nontrump card.
  • Every ordinary card of the chosen trump crop beats it.
  • If the Saboteur is led, every player may play any card; the Saboteur wins unless a trump is played.

Assignment and field failure

  • Because it matches every crop, its trick makes all four fields legal targets.
  • It contributes 0 work hours wherever it is assigned.
  • Any field with the Saboteur assigned as a Worker fails during requisition, even if that field reached quota and already paid its reward.

After the last trick

Banking

After four tricks, every player has one card remaining in hand. In Year 5, every player also has one card after the third trick. Place that card face-down in your Cellar.

The State collects

Requisition

1. Identify failed fields

A field fails if either condition is true:

  • Its assigned work total is below the applicable quota.
  • It has the Saboteur assigned as a Worker.

2. Identify vulnerable players

Every player holding one or more Medals is vulnerable. If one player won every Medal available that year, all players are vulnerable, including that Hero.

3. Build each player’s eligible pool

Resolve vulnerable players in turn order, starting to the Planner’s left. Consider every card in that player’s Plot and Cellar whose crop matches at least one failed field.

  • Cards gained this year, including claimed rewards and the card just banked, are eligible.
  • Face-down Workers in the Cellar are eligible.
  • The Saboteur matches every failed crop and is eligible at rank 0.

4. Take the Medal losses

Each vulnerable player sends their highest eligible card North once per Medal they hold. Recalculate after every card; an owner chooses between equally ranked cards. With no eligible card, nothing is lost for that Medal. If no field failed, nobody loses anything. Reveal any card sent North from a Cellar; otherwise Cellars stay hidden, and players are trusted to send their true highest eligible card. Return all Medals to the supply once requisition is finished.

Hero of Labor

If one player won every trick—four Medals, or three in Year 5—that player is the Hero of Labor (стахановец). Everyone, including the Hero, sends one highest eligible card North per Medal the Hero held at the start of requisition. Only the Hero returns Medals. The Hero has no immunity. If anyone runs out of eligible cards, the other players still complete their losses.

Years 1–4

End-of-year cleanup

  1. Return all four Medals to the shared supply.
  2. Send every unclaimed reward North. Gather every card assigned to a field, including any Ace or 2–5 that entered play through a swap, plus every undealt Worker. Shuffle these to form the next year’s Workers Deck. Reset all field totals to 0.
  3. Do not reshuffle cards in Plots, Cellars, or the North. Claimed rewards and banked Workers remain with their owners.
  4. Pass the Central Planner card to the player on the left.

Famine

Year 5

  1. Flip the Central Planner card to its Year 5 side.
  2. Deal four cards to each player.
  3. Do not reveal reward cards in Year 5.
  4. Reveal the top Worker and immediately send it North.
  5. The revealed Worker’s crop becomes trump. If it is the Saboteur, there is no trump for Year 5.
  6. Every player may make one optional hand swap, starting with the Planner and continuing left.
  7. Play three tricks, then bank the remaining card and resolve requisition normally.

After Year 5 requisition

Final scoring

  1. Add the printed ranks of every card remaining in your Plot and Cellar, including claimed rewards. The Saboteur scores 0.
  2. The player with the highest total wins.
  3. If tied, the tied player with more total cards in their Plot and Cellar wins.
  4. If score and card count are both tied, send those tied players North for greed. The player with the next-highest score wins. If every player is tied, nobody wins.

Medals do not score.

Medal cards track trick winners during the current year only. Return them after requisition; they are not retained for final scoring or tie-breaking.

Digital rules note

The app

The digital game currently differs from these physical base rules. Its default setup already includes 2–5 reward piles, starting Aces, Planner reward swaps, Year 1 hand swaps, and no Year 5 rewards. The committed engine reviewed for this update still adds competitive requisition comparisons and exempts the Hero, reshuffles unclaimed rewards, allows repeated reward swaps for a field, and uses different swap ordering and tie-breaking. It also has four seats only and uses a different crop identity for the starting Planner. The tutorial retains an older setup and swap sequence. Digital versions may change independently; the rules above govern the physical base game.
