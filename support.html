Digital game troubleshooting

Kolkhoz is designed for landscape play. If the interface appears cramped, rotate your device. The in-game menu also provides the tutorial, display settings, and clearer text options.

Full Game Unlock

Sign in to the Kolkhoz account used for the purchase, then choose Restore Purchase from the unlock dialog. One purchase unlocks the full game for that account on supported platforms. Purchases cannot currently be transferred between accounts.

Account deletion

In the app, open Profile and choose Delete account. If you cannot access the app, email support. Deleting an account permanently removes its associated data and surrenders its Full Game Unlock; the same storefront purchase cannot be linked to a newly created account.
