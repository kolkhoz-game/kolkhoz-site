How to Play

Five years. Four crops. One private score.

This page teaches the physical card game from the first deal through final scoring. For edge cases and exact definitions, use the complete rules.

Your objective

Build the richest private holding.

Cards in your face-up Plot and face-down Cellar score their printed ranks. Earn rewards by completing fields and bank one Worker after each year. The highest score after Year 5 wins.

Plot

Face-up claimed rewards. Everyone can see their crops and ranks.

Cellar

Face-down banked Workers. Only their owner may inspect them.

The North

Cards sent North stay face-up, are removed from play, and no longer score.

Choose the quota before dealing

With four players, a field needs 40 hours. With three players, it needs 32 hours. Nothing else about the deck or yearly structure changes.

The Five-Year Plan

One year at a glance.

Years 1–4 use five-card hands and four tricks. Year 5 uses four-card hands and three tricks. The same sequence—plan, play, assign, bank, requisition—drives every year.

1

Plan

Deal, reveal one reward per crop, choose trump, and make any allowed swaps.

2

Play a trick

Follow suit. Highest trump—or highest led crop—wins a Medal and leads next.

3

Assign the work

The winner assigns every card to any field whose crop appeared in the trick.

4

Complete fields

When a field reaches quota, the Brigadier who crossed it claims the reward.

5

Bank a Worker

After the last trick, put your remaining hand card face-down in your Cellar.

6

Resolve requisition

Failed fields determine how many eligible cards each Medal holder loses.

Wheat and Beet are the legal jobs.

Those are the only crops that appeared. The crop printed on an individual card does not restrict where that card can go.

2

You may split the trick.

Assign the Wheat 10 to Wheat and the three Beet cards to Beet. Cards are assigned one at a time and may be divided between legal jobs.

3

Or send all four cards to Wheat.

Wheat appeared, so even the Beet cards may work Wheat. The field already had 7 hours; this trick adds 33 and reaches the four-player quota of 40.

4

The crossing Brigadier claims the reward.

Because your assignment crosses 40, take the visible Wheat King into your Plot. The field remains open for later assignments and could still be poisoned by the Saboteur.

The Saboteur

  • It is always considered able to follow the led crop.
  • It is trump in every suit: it beats nontrumps, but every ordinary trump beats it.
  • If it leads, there is no ordinary lead crop; every player may play any card.
  • It makes all four fields legal assignment targets and contributes 0 hours.
  • A field containing it fails requisition even after reaching quota and paying a reward.

1

Count failed fields.

Beet finishes below quota, while Wheat, Sunflower, and Potato complete. One field failed, so N = 1.

2

Identify inspected players.

You hold a Medal because you won the worked trick. Every player holding one or more Medals is vulnerable, and loses one individual card per Medal.

3

Build your own eligible pool.

Check your Plot and Cellar for cards matching any failed crop. The Wheat King and Potato Jack are safe. The hidden Beet King from your swap is eligible.

4

Take one highest card per Medal.

Your highest eligible card—the Beet King—leaves your Cellar for the North before any comparison. The one competitive comparison then finds no card remaining.

5

Then compare once per failed field.

If Wheat and Sunflower fail, every target first loses one highest eligible card per Medal. For each failed field, targets reveal their highest remaining cards together and only the global highest goes North, including ties.

Hero of Labor

If one player wins every Medal that year, they are the Hero. Every other player loses one highest eligible card per Hero Medal. The Hero is protected and no competitive comparisons occur.

The final year

Year 5: Famine

Flip the Planner card to its Famine side. Deal four cards to every player and play only three tricks—but do not assume the year has no trump.

5

Year 5: Famine

Deal 4 each · reveal a Worker for trump · play 3 tricks

  1. Deal four cards to every player.
  2. Reveal one reward for each crop.
  3. Reveal the top remaining Worker and immediately send it North.
  4. That Worker’s crop is trump for Year 5.
  5. If the revealed Worker is the Saboteur, Year 5 has no trump.
  6. Players may make their normal Year 2–5 swap, then play three tricks.

After Year 5

Count every surviving card.

Add the printed ranks of every card in your Plot and Cellar, including claimed rewards. The Saboteur is worth zero.

1. Highest score

The greatest total rank wins.

2. Most cards

If scores tie, the player with more surviving cards wins.

3. Shared victory

If score and card count both tie, those players share the victory.
