The central tension

Work together. Score alone.

Each trick becomes a small negotiation. Its winner assigns every played card to the fields—but only to crops that appeared in the trick. Complete a quota and the Brigadier claims its reward in Years 1–4; Year 5 has no rewards. Leave fields unfinished and the State takes its share.

1

Take a trick

Follow the led crop. The highest trump—or highest card of the led crop—wins.

2

Assign the workers

The winner sends every card to any field whose crop appeared in that trick.

3

Face requisition

Medal holders send one highest eligible card North per Medal and return their Medals. If one player won them all, everyone—including that Hero—uses the Hero’s Medal count.

The four crops

Every suit is a field.

Printed rank is both work value and final score. A worker may be assigned away from their own crop, but only when the destination crop appeared in the trick.

The Saboteur

A rank-0 trump that can poison any field.

The Saboteur counts as every crop. You may always play it and must do so if it is your only match for the led crop. It makes every field legal during assignment and contributes zero hours. A field it works fails at requisition—even if that field already paid its reward. As a reward, it scores 0 without causing field failure.
